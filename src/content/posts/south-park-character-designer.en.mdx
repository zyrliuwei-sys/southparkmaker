A South Park-inspired character designer makes it easy to try a dozen outfits. Choosing between them is harder. Compare two drafts that differ in just one detail, then look at both at the size you plan to use them. This guide walks through that process with a trail-map reader in two raincoats.

## Give the design one job

Decide what the avatar needs to communicate. For a small profile picture, aim for a face that reads clearly. For a group scene, aim for a character you can spot among the others. The trail-map reader in this guide needs to work as a profile picture, so the bob and round glasses carry most of the design.

Open the [manual character designer](/create?mode=builder) and choose **The book lover** as a starting point. In **Outfit**, choose a raincoat and set its color to **Pine green**. In **Face**, choose **Chill** for a quiet expression. Use **Plain paper** in **Scene** so the background stays consistent during the comparison.

## Compare one change at a time

Use **Save on this device** to keep the first draft. Change the raincoat to **Charcoal plum**, then save the second draft. Keep the hair, glasses, expression, and background the same. If you change the silhouette and color together, it is harder to tell which edit improved the avatar.

![Two editor renders of the same bob-haired character on plain paper, wearing pine green and charcoal plum raincoats](/imgs/blog/character-designer-color-drafts.png)

Both drafts use the same character. The green coat adds a distinct area of color below the face. The plum coat gives the glasses and reddish bob more of the visual emphasis. For a sandy profile background, compare the plum version first. For a scene where the character needs to stand out among darker outfits, try the green one.

## Check the avatar at the size you will use it

Download both drafts as PNGs and open them side by side. Shrink the previews to around 64 pixels wide to approximate a small profile image. Check whether you can still pick out the glasses, tell the face from the hair, and recognize the coat color. If the face becomes hard to read, lighten the hair or try a clearer expression and run another comparison.

## Export the draft you picked

The manual designer exports a 1024 × 1024 PNG and a scalable SVG without an account. Use PNG for a profile upload and SVG when you want to resize the design later. For an avatar without the paper background, select **Transparent** in **Scene** before exporting.
