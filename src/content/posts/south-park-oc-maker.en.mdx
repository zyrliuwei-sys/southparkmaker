An OC is an original character you invent. Before opening a South Park-inspired OC maker, write one sentence about what the character does and one about what makes that difficult. Those two sentences give you something concrete to design.

## Start with a role and a small problem

Meet Milo. He repairs his neighbors’ bikes at the park after school. He is happy to help other people, but he gets flustered when he needs to ask for help himself.

That description suggests used work clothes and a bag for carrying tools. It also gives you two expressions to draw from the same character. He can look relaxed while fixing a bike and surprised when his own repair goes sideways.

To write your own brief, fill in three lines:

- What do other people come to this character for?
- What does this character struggle with?
- Where can you show both in one moment?

## Choose three details to carry across versions

Start with **The little explorer** in the [manual character builder](/create?mode=builder&preset=4). In **Hair & hats**, switch to **Spiky** and keep the **Beanie**. In **Outfit**, choose **Overalls** and **Lake blue**. In **Face**, add **Backpack**. Milo’s three visual anchors are the beanie silhouette, the blue overalls, and the bag. Keep those details recognizable when you change the pose or location.

The beanie takes on the outfit color in the editor, so the two pieces match in the builder renders below. The cover illustration explores a mustard beanie.

## Show a story beat with pose and expression

Set **Scene** to **Plain paper** for a clean reference. Save one draft with **Standing** and **Chill**. For a second draft, switch to **Wave** and **Surprised** to show Milo calling a friend over to help. The pose and expression change the moment while the clothing and silhouette keep him recognizable.

![Two builder renders of Milo in lake blue overalls and a beanie, first standing calmly and then waving in surprise](/imgs/blog/oc-maker-milo-poses.png)

Try **In the park** for a scene with more context. If you add a friend with **Add character**, give that character a different hair or hat shape so the two are easy to tell apart.

## Keep a reusable character reference

Choose **Save on this device** to reopen an editable draft in the same browser. Download a plain or transparent PNG and keep it with your two-sentence brief. If you plan to resize the art for a character sheet or story layout, keep the SVG too. You can use the manual builder and export either format without signing up.
