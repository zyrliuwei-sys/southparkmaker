Controls that leave room to create.

Use a 12px corner radius for actions and fields, 24px for primary panels, and a thin Mist border. Selected controls use Forest with a soft green surface. Hover moves an actionable card or button up by 2px; reduced-motion preferences remove that movement.

Focus is immediate: a 2px Forest outline with a 4px offset. Disabled controls remain legible and must be accompanied by an explanation in product flows. Do not replace real product output with a decorative fake interface.

A consistent studio, on every page.

Character direction

Original paper-cut characters carry the personality. Prefer live artwork from the builder for product previews and the original mountain illustration for supporting stories. Keep poses, faces, and clothing readable at small sizes.

Voice and language

Speak plainly, with curiosity and warmth. Name an action rather than promising a transformation. Explain the manual builder and optional AI workflow separately. Never invent ratings, users, or performance claims.

Spacing and hierarchy

Build on a 4px grid. Use 8, 16, 24, and 40px component spacing, with 104px section spacing on desktop and 68px on mobile. Default reading width stays comfortable; content should not become a wall of full-width text.

Light and dark

Light is the product default. Dark mode uses a forest-tinted canvas, a lighter accent, and the same token roles. Respect administrator appearance settings; never force a page-level theme over a user or administrator choice.
